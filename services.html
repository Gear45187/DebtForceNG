The Company


Debt Force is leader of the consortium of Asset Management Partners consisting of Debt Force, Leadville Solicitors, Godwin Kalu & Co. (an Estate Surveyor) and Gideon Nkwachi & Co (a firm of Chartered Accountants). The object of the consortium which currently partners with the Asset Management Corporation of Nigeria (AMCON) is to help resolve the Non Performing Loans in the banking system by deploying a collegiate approach that will prove to be effective and efficient.


Our value proposition is in the extensive Risk Management and overall Banking experience of our key promoters (cumulative forty five years) and the effectiveness and efficiency this delivers in executing our assignments. We also have the leverage of drawing on an extensive pool of highly motivated and committed cross disciplinary resources from within and outside the organization to carry out our assignments with clear objective of achieving results. We have considerable relationships with law enforcement agents, a wide pool of committed attorneys and pride ourselves with the deep seated know how from the vast Banking, Legal and accounting background of our promoters.

Debt Recovery Service


We have in the short period of our existence won big ticket recovery briefs from six Nigerian banks with a combined portfolio size of about N12 billion. We are happy to convey that our success rate for recoveries within this portfolio spectrum has been impressive and has probably outperformed the industry benchmark. Our success in this business segment is attributable to deployment of outside-the-box tools rarely found among professionals in the field who may not possess the cross disciplinary financial and negotiation skills required to elicit wholehearted co-operation from chronic debtors. We are primarily solution focused in our recovery initiatives which leads us into offering quality advisory that often carves a way out for debtors entangled in illiquidity traps. Our deployment of the instrumentality of coercion only occurs in instances where the debtor has proven recalcitrant even when there is established capacity to pay. In these instances, we have been very effective in using our cross functional resources to partner with the state apparatus in an effective manner as would lead to timely recovery of the delinquent portfolio.

Retail Debtors


This represents the most difficult cadre of debtors in our experience. Often, this category are out of business, having no cash flows, have depleted personal assets, are in declining businesses or are in new and struggling businesses after being shut out of the Banking System because of the debt debacle of their previous businesses. Retail debtors may also represent individuals who once had good jobs and were upwardly mobile but are now jobless and almost hopeless.

Our successes at AMCON on Retail Debtors


  1. Recovered chronic retail debts of many debtors already classified as lost in the books of the Corporation and/or the legacy banks which sold the debts to AMCON.
  2. Used out of the box tools to first locate these debtors who had gone underground and then enter into negotiated settlement arrangements with them.
  3. Followed through with the negotiated settlements to ensure that occasional defaults by the debtors do not occur and if they do, took corrective measures to redeem them in favor of payment.
  4. Size of the combined recoveries between 2010 and today estimated at N1.5 billion.

Commercial Debtors


Commercial debtors were a notch easier to deal with than retail debtors. However, the overflow in economic misfortunes of retail debtors caused by the economic meltdown from 2008 to 2010 had a ripple effect on commercial debtors and progressively weakened their fortunes. Commercial debtors may have collaterals securing the bad debt or not, may have associated businesses that still produce cash flow, may experience a resurgence or decline in existing business or may be out of business and hopeless.


Our successes at AMCON on Commercial Debtors


  1. We have and continue to be a foremost recovery agent achieving successes in recovery of core commercial debts in the books of AMCON and the legacy banks.
  2. Used out of the box negotiation, coercion and litigation resources to achieve recovery of these core debts.
  3. Size of combined recoveries estimated at N7 billion.

Corporate Debtors


Corporate debtors are easiest to deal with because of their size and longevity. The origin of their debt may arise from any of: disputation of debt, ability to pay but lack of willingness, preference to fund their business using spontaneous funds from creditors, or unnecessary bureaucracy.


Our successes at AMCON on Corporate Debts


  1. We have and continue to play at resolving these types of debt from a strict coercive, litigation and negotiation sense. However, we also resolve some of these debts from a restructuring and refinancing angle. Our client AMCON is very pleased with our endeavor.
  2. Size of these combined recoveries estimated at more than N35 billion.

We therefore request to be assigned accounts from your organization’s Non Performing Loan portfolio requiring effective recovery from defaulters. We assure you that we would deploy a wide array of options in the accomplishment of substantial success once the mandate is given to our firm.

Other Services We Provide


Enterprise Risk Management Advisory


The ERM Advisory arm of our business was engaged in 2015 by LAPO Microfinance Bank to articulate, create and implement a bank wide Enterprise Risk Management framework and acculturation project. That project commenced with our diagnosis of the traditional risk management practices as currently obtained in the bank, benchmarked against global best practice standards required of an Enterprise Risk Management practice. Having identified gaps of divergence between practice in the bank and requisite ERM standards, we designed a best-fit ERM framework suited to the envisioned going concern goals and long term business strategies of the MFB. Our work included ERM policy, systems, procedures, lines of authority, lines of defense, responsibilities, escalation mechanisms, disaster recovery mechanisms and other proactive measures. We rounded up our project in 2016 with recruitments into risk governance structures and charter creation for Executive Management and Board risk committees. We continue to intervene as called upon to smooth rough edges in the evolving ERM practice of the MFB.


Debt Resolution Advisory


The Debt Advisory arm of our business has seen substantial activity since inception of the company. We have advised highly indebted companies with debt profiles combined at more than N200 billion through negotiated settlements and schemes of arrangements resulting in the creditor banks and AMCON according substantial forbearance and concessions in addition to restructuring debt profiles over extended periods. Our effort in this respect has brought many companies back from the brink of extinction, resolved previously irreconcilable differences with Bankers and facilitated reclassification of debts from non-performing to performing.


Forensic Audits


We have developed in-house software to do forensic audit of bank statements of corporations and individuals to uncover excess and spurious charges by banks, including interest charges, COT charges or excess charges borne out of operational lapses, rent seeking or deliberate manipulation of the banking application. Our software has given a lie to the opinion that bank application software is error free. We leverage resident expertise not only to do forensic and investigative accounting on the bank statements of our clients, but also to create negotiation tools that leverage the outcome of our forensic exercise and obtain forbearance and debt extension arrangements in the best interest of our clients.
